# FrontierPass Website Requirements (V1)

## Project Overview

Design and develop the official website for **FrontierPass**.

This website should serve as the public face of the FrontierPass ecosystem and communicate our vision, credibility, and business to global enterprises, partners, and future collaborators.

The actual learning platform will exist separately under:

> app.frontierpass.net

This website is **not** the learning platform.

It is a premium corporate website designed to establish trust and introduce FrontierPass to the world.

---

# Objectives

The website should accomplish the following:

- Clearly explain what FrontierPass is
- Build trust with large enterprises and institutional partners
- Showcase real activities and execution on the ground
- Introduce our long-term vision
- Explain how organizations can collaborate with FrontierPass
- Direct students into the platform when necessary

The overall feeling should be:

> Quiet confidence.

Not flashy.

Not startup hype.

A company with an ambitious long-term vision that is already executing.

# Regional Websites

The homepage introduces FrontierPass globally.

Each country should have its own website.

Examples

- nigeria.frontierpass.net
- india.frontierpass.net
- japan.frontierpass.net

Regional websites should contain

- Local partnerships
- Activities
- Universities
- Community
- Local news
- Recruitment
- Programs

This structure allows FrontierPass to expand naturally into additional countries.
